Empowering Communities

Crafted with Love in Sri Lanka, Shared with the World.

Eco LOVE Lanka was born from a passion to protect Sri Lanka’s lush ecosystems while preserving ancient artisan crafts. Millions of discarded coconut shells are discarded daily. We intercept these natural gems, hand-sculpting them into stunning lightweight earrings, statement necklaces, bracelets, and key chains.

Combined with hand-loomed fabrics, genuine leather trims, and traditional batik textile art, each purchase directly provides sustainable livelihoods for rural women artisans.
